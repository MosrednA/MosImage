# Bria Fibo 2.0 edit-turbo versus current Klein 9B — 30 October 2026

Status: Completed isolated evaluation; not recommended for integration. Klein 9B remains Quality.

## Result

[Fibo 2.0 edit-turbo](https://huggingface.co/briaai/Fibo-2.0-edit-turbo) (8B, released 18–19 October 2026, first on the Artificial Analysis open-weights editing leaderboard) runs locally on the RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB after quantizing its transformer on load. On the eleven editing cases of the [FLUX 3 benchmark](FLUX3_KLEIN_2026-10-15.md), with two seeds each, all **66 renders** across three arms succeeded with zero changed protected pixels and no new `nvlddmkm` driver event.

It does not meet the bar set on 15 October (substantially faster at comparable quality, or clearly better at difficult edits):

- **Slower.** With cached prompts, NVFP4 Fibo takes a median **7.11 s** against Klein's **3.43 s**, and three to six times as long on cases with references, because Fibo encodes every reference at its own full trained size. A new prompt also needs a separate 8.3 GiB BF16 Qwen3-VL-4B encoder that does not fit beside the renderer within the 12.5 GiB cap.
- **Fails removal.** The masked kitchen removal puts a plate of food on the counter in all four Fibo renders (NVFP4 and FP8, both seeds). Klein removes the mug cleanly. Bria lists mask-based erasing as "coming soon".
- **Fills the whole grey box.** With a reference, the inserted mug and Anna grow to fill the entire masked rectangle. The mug is cut square with a broken handle; Anna gets a hard-edged background rectangle. Klein keeps both plausibly sized.
- **Better elsewhere.** A convincing roller-coaster car (Klein makes a bumper car), cleaner whole-image swords (Klein seed 42 grows a second crossguard), a clearer daisy, and good identity retention in the DJ edit. The glowing mushroom is larger and does not glow, where Klein follows "small" and "glowing".

FP8 and NVFP4 Fibo renders show the same behaviour in every case. The failures belong to the model, not to the quantization.

## Method

Use [`benchmark_fibo2.py`](../backend/tools/benchmark_fibo2.py) from the repository root, with no model loaded in the API:

```powershell
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pip install --no-deps --target models/research-cache/fibo2/pylib diffusers==0.42.1
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/tools/benchmark_fibo2.py --stage prepare
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/tools/benchmark_fibo2.py --stage encode
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/tools/benchmark_fibo2.py --stage fibo --quant nvfp4
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/tools/benchmark_fibo2.py --stage fibo --quant fp8
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/tools/benchmark_fibo2.py --stage klein9b
.\backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/tools/benchmark_fibo2.py --stage sheets
```

The weights (revision `9a24ee6810a782dd6aad4639fadcf48db0957ef4`, gated, CC BY-NC 4.0) are downloaded to `models/research-cache/fibo2/weights/`. `BriaFibo2Pipeline` needs diffusers 0.42. Diffusers 0.42.1 is installed without dependencies in a separate folder that only the Fibo stages put on `sys.path`; the app's environment stays at 0.41.0. Outputs, sheets and result JSON are under `data/fibo2-2026-10-30/`. Neither folder belongs in Git.

- **Cases and inputs.** The FLUX 3 fixtures with unchanged hashes, workspace crops, reference fit and 32-pixel grid. The typography case is skipped because edit-turbo cannot generate from text alone. Both models receive the application-style Klein prompts as plain edit instructions. There are no JSON captions and no Gemini prompt rewriting through `bria-vgl`.
- **Masks.** Fibo greys out the masked region before encoding and accepts a mask only with a single image. For the two masked cases with a reference (Anna, mug), the source is greyed out the same way and passed beside the reference without a mask. The shared compositor enforces the protected footprint in every arm.
- **Sampling.** Fibo uses 4 steps at guidance 1.0, as its model card recommends. Klein uses the current provider's 4 steps.
- **Quantization.** Fibo has no official NVFP4 or FP8 weights. The NVFP4 arm rounds every bias-free linear in the 44 main blocks to NVFP4 and runs it through the app's native Comfy Kitchen GEMM. The FFN's 64-aligned hidden rows are zero-padded to 128 and sliced off again. The activation scale is measured per call because no calibration exists. The FP8 arm stores the same linears as per-row FP8 and dequantizes them to BF16, as a quality reference. On random activations, the first layer's relative error against BF16 is 0.134 for NVFP4 and 0.027 for FP8.
- **Timing.** Fibo renders use prompt embeddings cached in a separate encode stage; the embeddings depend on the input images. Times include VAE work, sampling and both PNG writes. Compare Klein's seed-42 rows, which reuse its prompt cache.
- **Klein reproducibility.** All 22 Klein images are pixel-identical to the 15 October renders.

## Decision and limits

Not recommended: Fibo is about twice as slow, fails removal and box-filled insertion, and its gains on scene and whole-image edits are selective, like FLUX 3's. Its non-commercial licence is a further, minor obstacle. This test does not cover JSON-structured prompts, the 30-step Fibo 2.0 dev model, a calibrated or official quantization, or an FP8 encoder that might let encoder and renderer stay resident together. The model registry, defaults and desktop build are unchanged.
