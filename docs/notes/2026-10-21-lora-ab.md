# Community LoRAs on Quality, 21 October 2026

Status: Historical
Last verified: 2026-10-21
Current state: [Guide](../guide.md#your-own-loras).

**Recorded scope:** Two community LoRAs, tested through the user-LoRA support (`models/loras/`, `app/loras.py`) on the default profile: RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB, with Quality = Klein 9B NVFP4 at four steps and guidance 1. The jobs ran through `run_image_edit` and `run_inpaint` with a scratch data folder. Each case used seeds 7 and 42, PyTorch was capped at 12 GiB, and no `nvlddmkm` events occurred. Outputs stayed in a local scratch folder.

## Delight (linoyts/Flux2-Klein-Delight-LoRA, 9B, rank 32)

The setup was a whole-picture Edit with the LoRA's own instance prompt ("Relight the image to remove all existing lighting conditions … soft lightbox setup."). Four scenes (kitchen, phone, architecture, garden) ran with the prompt alone, then with Delight at 0.7 and at 1.0.

- **Prompt alone:** it already does most of the work. Sun patches, cast shadows and the warm colour cast go, on every scene.
- **Delight 1.0:** on top of that, it softens the remaining contact shadow (the kitchen mug) and gives a slightly brighter, higher-key result. It also moves colour further from the input. The mean colour shift on the kitchen scene was 21.4 with the prompt alone, 27.1 at 0.7 and 29.8 at 1.0.
- **Cost:** none measurable. Times were 7.5–8.2 s, the same as without the LoRA, and the peak stayed at 8.0 GiB.
- **Metric:** a luminance-spread metric (the standard deviation after a 24 px blur) did not separate the variants, because scene contrast dominates it.

**Verdict:** a useful optional LoRA at 1.0 for a flat "albedo" look. It is not a candidate for a built-in tool, because the prompt alone gets close.

## Remove-9B (trmz/flux2-klein-alpha `loras/remove_9b.safetensors`)

App Remove ran with no LoRA, then with Remove-9B at 0.7 and at 1.0, on four cases: a mug with its shadow on marble, a ball in a flower bed, a framed poster on brick, and a badge on a denim shirt.

The LoRA made no visible difference in any case. It cost 0.5–1 s and 0.5 GiB extra.

The reason is in its own demo: Remove-9B was trained on Klein **Base** 9B together with an RGBA VAE.
- The object is marked by alpha 128 in a four-channel input.
- It runs at 30 steps with guidance 4.

MosImage encodes RGB with the standard VAE on distilled 9B, so the mark never reaches the model. Its 9B weights are also under the FLUX Non-Commercial License.

**Verdict:** not usable here.

## Finding about app Remove

`Flux2KleinInpaintPipeline` always passes the source image as the first condition image (`ref_images = [image_latents_encoded]`). As a result, the model sees the object it is asked to remove, but it never sees the selection.

- **Large subjects:** these went cleanly on both seeds, including the mug's shadow and its reflection, and the ball.
- **Badge (about 40 px):** a tight ellipse mask left a faint ring where its edge had been. A mask about 1.3× wider brought the badge back almost unchanged, on both seeds, with and without the LoRA.
- **Framed poster:** the print went, but the frame came back inside the selection. With seed 42, the frame was filled with a mirror-like reflection.

## Fix: leave the selection out of the condition image

Two ways to hide the object from the condition image ran on the same cases, plus the wider badge and poster masks, on seeds 7 and 42:

- **Fill (a smooth fill from the surrounding colours, or flat grey):** the model copied the fill. The badge became a disc, and the mug and the wide poster area became blurry patches. Rejected.
- **Leave out:** MosImage drops the condition tokens that the selection covers (`_selection_left_out` in `app/models/flux2_klein.py`). Nothing of the object is left to copy, and there is no fill to copy either. Time and memory were unchanged (7–11 s, peak at most 7.9 GiB).

Results with leave-out:

- **Wide badge:** clean denim on both seeds. Before the fix, the badge came back almost unchanged.
- **Poster, tight and wide:** brick on both seeds, without the frame. On the wide poster, seed 42 leaves a small odd brick patch at the top left.
- **Tight badge:** the faint ring at its edge remains, as before. The selection hugs the badge's edge.
- **Ball in the flower bed:** clean. Seed 7 adds a soil path.
- **Mug on marble:** worse. This selection covers 63% of the crop the model sees; the other cases cover 3–23%. Without it, the counter's back edge stepped (seed 7) or grew a sink-like shape (seed 42), and the veins came out bolder and warmer.

**Applied rule:** Remove leaves the selection out unless it covers more than half of the crop. Above that, the full view stays, and that view removed the mug cleanly. The mug case therefore renders as before. Only one large case supports the threshold.
